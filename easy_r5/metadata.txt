description=Transit accessibility analysis with the Conveyal R5 routing engine.
about=Runs the Conveyal R5 routing engine (v7.6, MIT) as a child process to
    compute one-to-many and many-to-many travel-time matrices and
    cumulative-opportunity accessibility over a departure-time window, returning
    styled QGIS layers. R5 and a Temurin 21 JDK are downloaded on first setup —
    no pip, no R, no Docker, no administrator rights.
    Setup: DownloadR5 (Temurin 21 JDK + r5-v7.6-all.jar, SHA-256 verified),
    BuildNetwork (cached network.dat + network.json with per-date active trip
    counts). Diagnostics: TestR5Setup. Travel-time matrices, accessibility and
    isochrones arrive in later milestones.
